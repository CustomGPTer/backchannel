What Backchannel does not do

Both phones must be online for a message to cross, so a message can wait. There are no groups, no calls and no iPhone version. It has not had an independent security audit. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site.

Where the others do more

Briar can sync over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi with no internet, has groups, forums and blogs, and has been professionally audited. Session has voice and video calls and works when the other phone is offline. Both are on Google Play.
