Using Backchannel fairly

  • Use Backchannel on a phone or tablet that is yours, or that you have permission to manage.
  • Do not use Backchannel to break the law, to harass or threaten anyone, or to send anything it is unlawful for you to send or for the other person to receive.
  • Only add people who want to be added, and respect a request to stop.
  • Using Tor is restricted in some countries. Check the rules where you are.

Your PIN, passphrase and duress PIN

  • Your PIN or passphrase, your duress PIN and any backup passphrase are yours alone. The developer never sees them and cannot recover them for you.
  • If you forget your PIN or passphrase, the contents of the app cannot be recovered by anyone.
  • If you set a duress PIN, anyone who types it at the lock screen destroys everything in the app, at once, with no warning and no way back.
  • If you turn on fingerprint unlock, anyone whose fingerprint is registered on the phone can open the app's screens while it is running.

Your messages are your responsibility

  • What is in Backchannel is stored inside the app on your phone and nowhere else. Uninstalling it, clearing its storage, resetting the phone, or losing or breaking the phone deletes it.
  • Keeping a backup file, somewhere other than the phone, is up to you. The phone's own backup does not include Backchannel.
  • A backup must only be in use on one phone. Running the same backup on two phones will stop messages getting through on both.
  • A message is only delivered when both phones are online. Backchannel cannot promise that a message will arrive, or when.
  • What you send is then on the other person's phone, under their control.

What Backchannel cannot do

Backchannel encrypts your messages so that only the phone they are sent to can open them, and hides the route they take. It cannot protect you from software that has control of your phone or your contact's phone, from someone holding either phone unlocked, from a PIN that is easy to guess, or from the person you are talking to. It does not hide the fact that you are using Tor. Backchannel is at an early version and has not had an independent security review. Do not rely on Backchannel alone where a failure would put someone in danger.
