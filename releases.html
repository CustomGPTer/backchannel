Release notes

What changed in each version of Backchannel. The newest is at the top.

Version 0.3 19 November 2026Latest

  • First public release. One-to-one messages encrypted end to end with the Signal Protocol (libsignal), including its post-quantum key exchange.
  • No server: each phone runs Tor and has its own onion address, and messages go directly from one phone to the other.
  • Adding a contact by scanning a code or pasting an invite. Each code adds one person and stops working once used, or after seven days.
  • Messages are saved first and sent when the other phone can be reached. Each is marked Waiting, Delivered or Not delivered.
  • Photos from the gallery or the camera, shrunk and stripped of location and all other hidden details, with a preview before sending.
  • Voice notes of up to three minutes.
  • Disappearing messages with a timer per chat, from five minutes to four weeks, shared by both phones.
  • An encrypted database (SQLCipher). Its key is locked with your PIN or passphrase (Argon2id) and again with a key in the phone's security hardware.
  • A PIN or a passphrase, with a strength rating as you choose, and growing waits after wrong tries.
  • Fingerprint for opening the screens while the app is running. The PIN or passphrase is always needed after a restart.
  • A choice of when the screens lock: straight away, after one minute or after five.
  • A duress PIN that wipes every message, contact and key.
  • An encrypted backup file with its own passphrase, and restore at first setup. After a restore the phone sets up fresh keys with each contact by itself.
  • A contact's name and picture copied from one address-book entry you choose, with no permission to read the address book.
  • Notifications that say only New message. Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked.
  • A check for newer versions, made over Tor, once a day and on demand.
  • Dark, light and follow-the-phone themes.

Checking your download

The file for version 0.3 is called Backchannel.apk. It is 35 MB (35,080,678 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

277a9348cfd948e019a1f2141c69fd1c73fa87688203010fbab4b0e2d0c4e6f0

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Backchannel.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
